# How to Get an ArNS Domain

ArNS (Ar.io Name System) allows you to register a human-readable domain for the Arweave ecosystem.

## Connect Your Solana Wallet

Go to the official ArNS website:

https://arns.ar.io/

Connect your Solana wallet.

## Getting a Domain Name

Enter the domain name you want to register.

If the name is available, click **Register**.

You can choose between two registration options:

- **Lease:** Register the domain for a period between 1 and 5 years.
- **Buy:** Purchase the domain permanently.

The current registration cost is displayed on the page in USD and ARIO.

Choose your preferred option and, if you selected Lease, set the registration period.

Click **Next**, review the transaction and confirm it with your Solana wallet.

Once the transaction is completed, your ArNS domain is registered.
