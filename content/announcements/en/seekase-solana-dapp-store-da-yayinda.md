Seekase, the application I have been working on for some time, is now available on the Solana dApp Store. Seeker users can find and install it by searching for **Seekase** in the store.

[Visit the Seekase website](https://seekase.app/)

## What is Seekase?

Seekase is a mobile application I built for people who want to organize their collections digitally and share them with other collectors.

A collection may consist of books, old coins, or cameras gathered over many years. It may also begin with only a few objects. Where those objects came from, why they were kept, and what they mean to their owner often remain only in the collector's memory. I built Seekase to provide a more organized way to record that information alongside photographs and share it when the collector chooses.

![The Seekase Discover screen](/assets/images/blog/seekase-discover.png)

## What can you do in Seekase?

You can create your own collections and record individual objects with photographs and stories. Seekase includes a broad category structure for books, coins, stamps, comics, art, cameras, retro technology, and many other kinds of collectibles.

The Discover section lets you browse shared collections, follow other collectors, and like or save objects that interest you. Comments and direct messages provide ways for collectors to speak with one another inside the application.

Seekase is not a marketplace or a payment application. Its purpose is to give collections a place to be presented and to preserve the information behind each object.

![A collection page in Seekase](/assets/images/blog/seekase-collection.png)

## Seeker and Solana Mobile

Solana Seeker was my first priority while developing Seekase. The application can connect to compatible wallets through Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter. Wallet information remains separate from the public collector profile; only the relevant verification badges are shown publicly.

Seekase also includes Seeker Genesis Token verification and a daily check-in. Collection and social features that do not require a wallet remain available through a Seekase account.

![Collection categories in Seekase](/assets/images/blog/seekase-categories.png)

## The first published release

The version published on the Solana dApp Store is **1.0.1**, with build number **3**. I tested this release as a standalone APK on a physical Seeker device. It can now be installed directly from the store, and future updates can be delivered through the same release process.

Feedback from collectors will be important from this point onward. If you use a Seeker, you can try Seekase and tell me about any problems you encounter, anything that feels incomplete, or features you would like to see in a future version.

Search for **Seekase** in the Solana dApp Store to install it.
