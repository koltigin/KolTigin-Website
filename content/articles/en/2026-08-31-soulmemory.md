I wanted a small, personal app that left a durable mark for a feeling that usually disappears by the next day. SoulMemory is that experiment: an on-chain mood diary, built as a Mini App on Base and shared through Farcaster.

[Open App](https://soul-memory.vercel.app/)

## The idea

Most of the things I record online sit in a feed, a note, or a screenshot. They are easy to lose and hard to treat as mine. SoulMemory started from a simpler question: what if a single daily mood lived on-chain, as a personal record I could keep?

The name is the brief. A soul-level memory, written down for that day, not as a social post but as something that stays with the wallet.

## Why a mood diary?

A mood is small enough to capture in a moment and specific enough to mean something later. I did not want a long journal or a productivity tracker. I wanted one honest snapshot: how the day felt.

A diary like that is private in spirit even when the record is public on a chain. It is not a performance. It is a trail.

## Why on-chain?

If the point is memory, the record has to outlast the app screen. Putting the daily mood on-chain turns it into an NFT you hold, not a row in someone else's database that can vanish with a product shutdown.

I am not claiming that every feeling belongs on a blockchain. For this idea, the chain is the notebook: a date, a mood, a memory that remains attached to you.

## Why Base, and why a Mini App?

SoulMemory is built around Base, and it was shaped as a Mini App so it could be opened and used in the Farcaster context people already inhabit. That mattered more to me than building another isolated website that nobody opens twice.

Base was the chain I wanted the memories to live on. The Mini App format was how I wanted people to reach them.

## How SoulMemory works

From the user's side, the path is short — the same three steps the app itself lists:

1. Connect a wallet.
2. Choose a mood (Happy, Sad, Angry, Calm, Tired, Excited, or Anxious) and an energy reading from −5 to +5. You also write a short note, up to 140 characters.
3. Mint that day's SoulMemory as an NFT on Base.

Each mint is meant as one day's mark. Over time those marks form a personal on-chain memory trail rather than a feed of posts.

## What I learned while building it

Building a small product around a feeling is harder than it sounds. The interface has to stay quiet. The action has to feel like writing a line in a diary, not filling a form. Shipping it as a Mini App also meant thinking about how someone discovers it inside Farcaster, not only how the mint itself works.

I also learned to stop before the story got bigger than the object. SoulMemory is a mood, a day, and a record. That is enough for a first version.

## What could come next

I am not attaching a roadmap to this. If the diary stays useful, it can grow. If it does not, the memories that were written still sit on-chain. That was the point of putting them there.
