Digital University Platform Applications

Applications for the 2026–2027 academic year of Türkiye's Digital University Platform are now open. The program offers free online courses in artificial intelligence, data analysis, and sustainability, with instructors from five Turkish universities.

The original application deadline of October 20 has been extended to October 26, 2026, at 23:59 Türkiye time (UTC+3).

What Is the Digital University Platform?

The Digital University Platform is an educational initiative operating under the auspices of Türkiye's Council of Higher Education (YÖK). It aims to make university-level education accessible to people interested in developing their knowledge and technical skills.

The program is coordinated by:

  • Marmara University, Institute of Population and Social Research
  • Yıldız Technical University

Three additional universities contribute to the academic program:

  • Boğaziçi University
  • Istanbul Technical University (İTÜ)
  • Middle East Technical University (METU)

Applications and participation are free of charge.

Available Courses

The 2026–2027 program includes 16 modules organized into three schools. Applicants may select up to two modules.

1. Artificial Intelligence School

The Artificial Intelligence School offers three modules:

  1. Artificial Intelligence: Machine Learning — Fundamental concepts of machine learning, neural networks, deep learning, and reinforcement learning.
  2. Artificial Intelligence: Facilitating Tools — Practical applications of AI tools, including ChatGPT, Claude, and similar technologies.
  3. Modern Software Development — Software development practices, project development, and AI-assisted programming.

These courses may be particularly relevant to people interested in building applications, working with AI models, or developing their programming skills.

2. Data Analysis School

The Data Analysis School includes seven modules:

  1. Basic Statistics
  2. Panel Data
  3. Computational Social Sciences
  4. Digital Humanities
  5. Psychometrics
  6. Biostatistics
  7. Data Analytics

The courses cover statistical methods and data analysis techniques used in different research fields. Depending on the module, participants may work with programming languages such as R and Python.

3. Green Transformation and Sustainability School

Six modules are available in this school:

  1. Climate Change and Global Policies
  2. Corporate Sustainability
  3. Environmental Sustainability
  4. Circular Economy and Sustainable Finance Ecosystem
  5. Social Sustainability
  6. Clean Energy Technologies

The modules address environmental issues, energy technologies, and sustainability from different perspectives.

The full schedule and course descriptions are available on the official program website.

Who Can Apply?

According to the published general requirements, applicants must be at least 18 years old. No general academic prerequisite is specified.

University students, graduates, researchers, professionals, and people interested in learning new skills can apply.

The application form requests personal and educational information, English proficiency details, and a motivation letter explaining the applicant's interest in the program.

Applicants may choose a maximum of two modules. Places are limited, and submitting an application does not guarantee admission.

Can International Applicants Participate?

The platform provides its website and application interface in Turkish, English, and Arabic. The application form also supports international telephone country codes.

These features suggest that the application system is designed to accommodate users outside Türkiye.

However, the published information does not explicitly confirm whether all foreign nationals are eligible for admission. International applicants should verify any nationality or residency requirements before submitting their applications.

The availability of an English-language website also does not necessarily mean that every course will be taught in English. Applicants should check the language of instruction with the organizers.

Program Dates and Certificates

The courses are scheduled to begin on November 3, 2026. The program is expected to run for approximately 24 weeks across two terms.

Classes will be held online.

Participants who meet the attendance requirements will receive a certificate of participation. Those who also achieve an average score of at least 50 on the short assessments will qualify for a certificate of achievement.
